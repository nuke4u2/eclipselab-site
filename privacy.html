Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Eclipse Lab operates personal software and home-automation projects, including Home Automation Notifications.

Google user data

Home Automation Notifications uses Google OAuth authorization solely to send automated email notifications through Gmail.

The application requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send permission. It does not request access to read, modify, delete, or search Gmail messages.

How data is used

Google authorization data is used only to authenticate requests to send notification email. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sale of personal information.

Storage

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on systems controlled by the application owner and are not published in source-code repositories.

Sharing

Google user data and OAuth credentials are not sold or shared with third parties, except as required for communication with Google's services to provide the email-sending functionality.

Revoking access

Google account authorization may be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings.
